    A better way to plan Cyprus

    You've booked your flight. Now what? Instead of juggling separate websites, guidebooks, and scattered recommendations, open one app that knows the island intimately. CyprusWay helps you discover curated places, preview them in immersive 360° virtual tours, build personalised itineraries, ask a friendly travel companion questions, and arrange your bookings — hotels, car rental, activities, and transfers — through trusted travel providers.

    What the app offers

    Four capabilities for your trip

    🌍

    360° Virtual Tours

    Walk through places before you visit. See beaches, archaeological sites, and mountain villages in full 360° from your phone. Each tour comes with narration so you understand what you're seeing. Explore at your own pace, in your own language.

    🗺

    Trip Planning

    Build personalised itineraries shaped by your interests, travel pace, and time. Tell the app where you're staying and what excites you — beaches, history, food, nature, nightlife — and it builds a day-by-day plan with practical details like opening hours and driving distances.

    💬

    Ask Pete

    A friendly travel companion who knows Cyprus. Ask about beaches, history, food, getting around, and the honest practical details that matter. Pete answers confidently when he knows, adds a caveat when things may have changed, and politely redirects you when he doesn't have an answer — never fabricating.

    🏨

    Booking Help

    Find and compare hotels, rental cars, activities, and airport transfers. Answer a few simple questions, and CyprusWay connects you directly to trusted travel providers with your filters applied. All four capabilities →

    Where are you headed?

    Six distinctive regions

    Paphos

    Ancient UNESCO heritage, Roman mosaics, medieval harbour, and the mythical birthplace of Aphrodite.
    Limassol

    Cosmopolitan waterfront, modern marina, medieval castle, and the gateway to Cyprus's wine country.
    Larnaca

    Palm-lined seafront, salt lake flamingos, a 9th-century church, and one of the world's top wreck dives.
    Ayia Napa

    Golden beaches, crystal-clear water, Cape Greco National Park, sea caves, and the Med's most energetic nightlife.
    Protaras

    Blue Flag beaches, sheltered coves, Fig Tree Bay, and a relaxed family atmosphere on the eastern coast.
    Troodos

    Byzantine churches, cedar forests, mountain villages, waterfalls, and cool air — a different Cyprus entirely.

    CyprusWay helps you find and compare hotels, rental cars, activities, and airport transfers.
