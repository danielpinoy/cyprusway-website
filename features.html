    What CyprusWay does

    Four capabilities built around how you actually plan a trip

    01

    360° Virtual Tours

    Before you commit to visiting a place, see it first. CyprusWay offers immersive 360° virtual tours of key destinations across the island. Walk through an archaeological site, stand on a beach at sunset, explore a mountain monastery — all from your phone. Each tour is accompanied by audio narration so you understand what you're seeing.

    The tours are shot on location across Cyprus, covering beaches, historical sites, nature trails, and cultural landmarks. You can explore them at your own pace, switching between languages as needed. Virtual tours help you build genuine anticipation and make smarter decisions about where to spend your holiday time.
