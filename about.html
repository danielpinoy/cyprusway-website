    About CyprusWay

    A travel companion for the island we love

    CyprusWay is a mobile travel app built to help travellers experience Cyprus with confidence. We believe that a great trip starts with good information — knowing what a place actually looks like, whether it suits your pace and interests, and how to get there. The app brings together curated places, immersive 360° virtual tours, personalised trip planning, a friendly travel assistant, and booking help — so you can discover, plan, and arrange your entire Cyprus holiday in one place.

    The idea behind CyprusWay is simple: you've already booked your flight to Cyprus. Now what? Instead of juggling separate websites, guidebooks, and recommendations from strangers, you open one app that knows the island well and helps you make confident decisions — from which beach to visit on your first morning to how to book your hire car at Larnaca Airport.

    CyprusWay is a product of Almisource LTD, a company focused on building practical, content-rich travel tools for specific destinations. We are not a global booking platform — we are a focused Cyprus travel companion.

    What the app covers

    CyprusWay covers curated places across the island — beaches, archaeological sites, monasteries, nature trails, villages, wineries, and more. Each place includes photos, descriptions, opening hours, practical tips, and location information. The app currently supports five languages: English, Polish, German, Greek, and Swedish.

    CyprusWay's booking feature helps users find and compare hotels, car rental, activities, and airport transfers through trusted travel providers. CyprusWay may earn a commission from bookings made through these links, at no extra cost to you.
